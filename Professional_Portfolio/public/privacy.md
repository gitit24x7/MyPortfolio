# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** January 2025

## Overview
This portfolio website (adityaojha.vercel.app) is a personal portfolio site owned and operated by Aditya Ojha. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected and used when you visit this website.

## Information We Collect

### Automatically Collected Information
- **Visitor Count:** We use a third-party counter service (CounterAPI) to track the total number of visitors. This is aggregated data only and does not identify individual users.
- **Local Storage:** We store a simple flag (`visit_counted`) in your browser's localStorage to prevent duplicate counting. This is not personal data.

### Analytics
This site may use Vercel Analytics for understanding traffic patterns. This data is anonymized and aggregated.

### No Personal Data Collection
We do **NOT** collect:
- Names or email addresses (unless you contact us directly)
- IP addresses for tracking
- Cookies for advertising or tracking
- Any personally identifiable information

## Third-Party Services
- **Vercel:** Hosting and deployment
- **CounterAPI:** Anonymous visitor counting
- **CDN Services:** For loading fonts and icons

## Your Rights
Since we don't collect personal data, there's nothing to delete or export. If you've contacted us via email, you can request deletion of that correspondence.
